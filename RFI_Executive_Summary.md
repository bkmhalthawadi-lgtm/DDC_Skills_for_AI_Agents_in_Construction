## SECTION 1 — IMPACT ANALYSIS: MEP–CIVIL CLASHES & DEPENDENCIES

### 1.1 Overview

The RFI log reveals a systemic pattern of **MEP services conflicting with structural elements** (beams, drop panels, and shafts) across floors 1F through 12F and Basements B1–B2. The dominant themes are:

- **Insufficient shaft sizes** unable to accommodate combined MEP riser services (drainage + chilled water)
- **Unauthorised pipe penetrations** through structural beams requiring sleeve approvals
- **Floor drain/floor trap placement** clashing with structural drop panels and tile joint layouts
- **Incomplete coordination** between MEP sub-contractor, interior designer (ID), and structural engineer

### 2.2 General RFI Writing Guidelines

1. **State the problem quantitatively** — include pipe diameters, sleeve sizes, dimensions, and grid references. Avoid "highlighted" without a reference number.
2. **Separate the question from the background** — use distinct "Background" and "Question / Action Required" sections.
3. **Set a response deadline** — always include "Response required by [date] to avoid impact to [activity]."
4. **Use sentence case, not ALL CAPS** — all-caps text is harder to scan and appears informal to design consultants.
5. **Reference related RFIs explicitly** — e.g., "Refer RFI ARC_041 Pt. 5 for precedent approval" rather than "as per previous."
6. **Attach marked-up drawings as standard** — every RFI should reference a sketch or drawing excerpt; avoid "as per attached" without a drawing number.

## SECTION 5 — KEY OBSERVATIONS

1. **Systemic MEP–Civil Interface Failure:** The volume of beam penetration rejections (ARC_056, ARC_057, ARC_058) suggests MEP shop drawings were not sufficiently coordinated with structural drawings before construction commenced. A formal **clash-detection workshop** using 3D BIM/Navisworks is strongly recommended before proceeding to upper floors (13F+).

2. **Recurrent "Design Discussion On-going" Status:** RFIs ARC_042 and ARC_054 remain open with no resolution timeline. Open design discussions during construction are a primary cause of programme overruns. These must be formally closed with a consultant instruction within 72 hours.

3. **Repeated Cross-References Instead of Resolution:** Several RFIs reference earlier RFIs without closing the loop (ARC_055 → ARC_040; ARC_065 → GEN_026). This creates a chain-dependency risk where an error in the base RFI propagates to all referencing floors.

4. **ID Coordination as a Blocker:** The phrase "NEED TO COORDINATE WITH ID" appears in 14 of 27 RFIs. Interior design sign-off is a mandatory prerequisite for MEP finalisation. A dedicated **MEP-ID weekly coordination register** should be established immediately.

5. **DM Submission Risk:** RFI ARC_059 explicitly notes that sleeve locations must be reflected in PT (Post-Tensioned) drawings **prior to DM submission**. Any delay in resolving structural sleeve positions could hold the DM approval cycle for the entire floor plate.
